题目：Write a short paragraph about your favorite season and explain why you like it.

My favorite season is autumn. I like it because the weather is cool and comfortable. The leaves turn yellow and red, it is very beautiful. In autumn, I can eat many delicious fruits like apples and grapes. I also like to go hiking with my family in the mountain. The air is fresh and the sky is blue. Autumn make me feel happy and relax. I think autumn is the best season in the year.
