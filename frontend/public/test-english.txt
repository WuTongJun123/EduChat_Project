Topic: Write a short essay (about 150 words) about your favorite season.

My Favorite Season

My favorite season is autumn. In autumn, the weather become cool and comfortable after the hot summer. The leaves on the trees change color, turning into beautiful shades of red, orange and yellow. I love walking in the park and see the falling leaves.

Another reason I like autumn is because of the Mid-Autumn Festival. My family always gather together to eat mooncakes and admire the full moon. It is a very warm and happy time.

Also, autumn is the season of harvest. Farmers work hard and finally get their reward. The fruits and vegetables in this season is very fresh and delicious. I especially enjoy eating persimmons and pomegranates.

In conclusion, autumn bring me beautiful scenery, family reunions and delicious food. That is why it is my favorite season of all.
